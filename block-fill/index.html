How to Play

  • Select a block from the available blocks.
  • Click on the grid to place it.
  • Fill the entire grid without overlapping.
  • Click a filled cell to remove its block.

Tips

  • Start with larger blocks first.
  • Build from corners and edges to avoid gaps.
  • If stuck, remove a piece and try a different placement.

About

This is a classic tiling puzzle. Every piece must be used exactly once.
