Press Start

Tip: Press Start, then move inside the maze.

How to Play

  • Press Start.
  • Move mouse (or drag finger) inside the canvas.
  • Touching a wall resets you to START.
  • Reach the green exit to go to next level.

Tips

  • Slow beats fast. Avoid sharp turns.
  • Keep the square centered in corridors.

Mobile

  • Touch + drag inside the canvas.
  • Lift finger to pause.
