# Zoo Escape

Owner and builder: Claude. Slug: `zoo-escape`. Skill game 19 (order 29), complexity 4, wave 3. Spec: `docs/arcade-expansion/04-game-specs-skill.md` §19 (+ 03 "Conventions" / "Common Definition of Done", 05 §E.4 row 19). Gate G0 design on `claude/design-zoo-escape`; nothing built yet. Units: metres, seconds; x east, z south, y up; the panda escapes **north (−z)**. Yaw as `ai/vision`: 0 faces +z, positive towards +x. Every "Changed from spec" line says what and why.

## Concept

A cheeky panda breaks out of the zoo in three connected areas (enclosure → visitor plaza → parking lot at dusk). Keepers walk fixed patrol loops with **visible flashlight cones**; the panda slips between bushes, sneaks past within earshot, tosses a bamboo bundle to lure a keeper away and grabs bamboo snacks on risky detours. Stealth for families: everything a keeper can see is drawn on the floor, and every stage has a safe route at every patrol phase (proven by a solver). Accent `#34d399`.

## Controls

`meta.ts`: `scheme: "joystick"`, `touchControls: ["joystick", "jump", "action"]`, `touchLabels: { jump: "Sneak", action: "Toss" }`.

- `keyboard`: "WASD / arrows to move, hold Space to sneak, E or Enter to toss bamboo". Changed from the stub ("E to toss a snack") because snacks are what you collect; the toss is a bamboo bundle.
- `touch`: "Joystick to move, hold Sneak to sneak, Toss to throw bamboo" (the buttons carry those labels).
- Input fields: `moveX` / `moveY` → `inputToWorld(moveX, moveY, view.yaw)` (up = away from the camera = north); sneak = held `jump` (Space / touch Sneak); toss = `actionPressed` (E / Enter / touch Toss, one-frame, never lost). `jumpPressed`, `pressed`, `swipe`, `tap`, `tapDown` are unused. Esc / P pause (shell).

## Rules

All numbers in `rules.ts` (`PANDA`, `KEEPER`, `VISION`, `HEARING`, `SUSPICION`, `TOSS`, `CATCH`, `CLOCK`, `SEARCHLIGHT`) and `level.ts` (geometry), pinned in tests. Logic runs in one `useRunFrame`; `stepRun(run, input, dt)` is pure and seeded (`createRun(seed, { coarse })`).

- **Map** (`level.ts`, hand-authored; the seed picks only patrol phases and snack spots). Stage 1 enclosure x ∈ [−7, 7], z ∈ [0, 18]: start (−4.5, 16.5); moat band z ∈ [3, 5] (solid) crossed by a bridge x ∈ [−1, 1]; rock clusters, bamboo clumps, 4 bushes; gate G1 in the north fence, x ∈ [3, 5]. Stage 2 plaza x ∈ [−9, 9], z ∈ [−20, 0]: entry (4, −1.5); benches, bins, lamps, 2 kiosks, palms, 2 leafy trees, 5 bushes; gate G2 x ∈ [−6, −4] at z = −20. Stage 3 parking x ∈ [−10, 10], z ∈ [−42, −20]: entry (−5, −21.5); 8 parked cars in two rows, lamps, 4 planter bushes, searchlight tower at (10.8, −31); exit gate G3 x ∈ [5, 8] at z = −42.
- **Footprints**: solids (block movement) = rocks, bamboo clumps, moat, kiosks, tree trunks, palm bases, benches, bins, lamp posts, cars, fences (thin AABBs along the kit paths), tower. **Sight blockers** (`hasLineOfSightXZ` boxes) = the tall solids (rocks, bamboo, kiosks, trunks, cars, tower) **and every bush**; benches, bins, lamps and fences are low or thin and do not block sight. Bushes (AABBs ≥ 1.6 × 1.6) are walkable for panda and keepers.
- **Panda** (`PANDA`): circle r 0.5; walk 3.2 m/s, sneak 1.6 m/s (Space / Sneak held); accel 20, brake 28, `turnTowards` 12/s; `resolveSphereAabb` against solids, `clampToBounds` to the **map box** x ∈ [−10, 10], z ∈ [−44, 18] (the union's outer box; stage 1's narrower walls, the walls between stages and the gate posts are fence solids, so the gate gaps are the only way through), then the step is scaled back to `speed · dt` (no push ever adds speed). Facing = last move direction.
- **Hidden**: the panda's centre inside a bush's (full) box. One box for everything: vision (the sight segment ends inside a blocker, so `hasLineOfSightXZ` is false by construction), the bush search, the outline and the rustle; the drawn leaves overhang the box by 0.3 m so a hidden panda looks covered. Re-entering a bush is free and unlimited. **A keeper is never blind in a bush**: each keeper's blockers exclude the bush box that contains its own feet (rules and C1), since a segment starting inside a box is blocked.
- **Keepers** (`KEEPER`): stage 1 one, stage 2 two (crossing loops), stage 3 three; circle r 0.35. `core/ai` `stepPatrol` on closed, unsmoothed `createPath` routes (each corner is a waypoint), speed 1.3 m/s patrolling and returning (`mode` "patrol" / "return"), 2.0 investigating (the game passes the options for the current mode; the keeper stores its investigation **kind** `seen | heard | bamboo | radio` and passes that kind's `investigateWait`, 2.5 / 2.0 / 3.0 / 2.5 s, since `stepPatrol` reads it on arrival), `wait` 1.2 s at every corner with `lookAngle` 0.6 rad, `lookSpeed` 2.2. All keepers of a stage share one cycle `C` (stage 1 27 s, stage 2 24 s, stage 3 18 s, multiples of 1.5 s so each offset is a whole number of 0.25 s solver steps; route lengths and waits tuned to it, pinned). Seeded phase per keeper: `C · k / 6`, k ∈ 0..5 (18 / 16 / 12 steps) (so a stage has 6 / 36 / 216 phase combinations, all checked by the solver). Every keeper patrols from the run's start; only the **current stage's** keepers (and searchlight) detect.
- **Zones**: each keeper has a convex AABB zone that contains its route and **no solid**. Every investigation target is clamped into the zone (`clampToBounds`), so straight walks (to the target, and `stepPatrol`'s straight return to the nearest route point) never cross a solid. Keepers may walk through bushes. Keepers do not collide with each other (they pass through; routes are tuned so they rarely meet); no `ai/steering`. Changed from spec (dependency `ai/steering`): not needed for straight patrols in solid-free zones.
- **Vision** (`VISION`): `inViewCone(feet, yaw, 30°, range, panda)` (60° cone) and `hasLineOfSightXZ(feet, panda, blockers)` and not hidden. Range 7 m in stages 1–2, **9 m in stage 3**. Changed from spec ("7 m → 9 m"): read as the spec's "longer cones" of stage 3, constant within a stage. During a wait the cone sweeps with `stepPatrol`'s look-around (`agent.yaw`).
- **Hearing** (`HEARING`): a moving panda (speed > 0.2) within 3.0 m of a keeper's feet (1.2 m while sneaking), hidden or not (moving in a bush rustles), is heard: that keeper `investigate`s the panda's position at that frame (no meter fill). A still panda is never heard. One hearing per keeper per 2 s.
- **Suspicion** (`SUSPICION`, a meter per keeper, 0..1): +dt / `catchS` while it sees the panda (`catchS` 1.2 s; **1.5 s on a coarse pointer**, read once at mount and passed to `createRun`); −0.5/s otherwise. 0 → > 0 shows "?"; losing sight with 0 < s < 1 → `investigate(last seen point)` (wait 2.5 s, look-around 0.7 rad), then back to its route (core behaviour). s ≥ 1 → **caught** ("!"). Two keepers seeing at once fill their own meters (not added). Priority of a keeper's target: seen > heard > bamboo.
- **Bump and bush search**: a keeper's circle overlapping the panda's circle = caught at once (even from behind). A keeper whose circle overlaps the box of the bush the panda hides in = caught at once ("found"). At most one bush per stage lies on a patrol route; the HUD outline warns (Accessibility).
- **Searchlight** (`SEARCHLIGHT`, stage 3): tower at (10.8, −31), base yaw −π/2 (facing west), sweep `yaw = base + 0.95 · sin(2π (t + phase) / 9)` (9 s period, half of `C3`; phase from keeper 1's), half angle 12°, range 14 m, same sight rule and blockers. It has its own meter (same fill and decay); full = caught; sight lost with 0 < s < 1 → the nearest stage-3 keeper within 12 m investigates the last seen point. It never moves and ignores bamboo; its meter resets to 0 on a catch.
- **Toss** (`TOSS`): 2 bamboo bundles per stage. `actionPressed` with bundles left and none in flight throws one 4.0 m ahead along the facing; the landing is the last free point on that segment (0.1 m march; 0.3 m short of a solid or the stage edge). Flight 0.6 s: `ballistics.launchForTime(from = panda + 0.6 y, to, 0.6, { gravity: 9.81 })` gives the velocity; the arc is drawn in closed form and the rules land it exactly at 0.6 s ("thud"). On landing, the nearest current-stage keeper within 10 m of it (ties: lower index) with meter 0 investigates it (clamped to its zone; wait 3.0 s, look-around 0.7). A keeper already on a bamboo is re-targeted by a newer one. No keeper in 10 m, or all suspicious: nothing (bundle spent). Landing in the panda's own bush brings the keeper into it: found (consistent, the player's choice). Bundles stay on the ground until a catch or the stage is left, then are hidden (pool of 2).
- **Snacks**: 4 per stage (12 per run) picked by the seed from 7 hand-placed candidates per stage; never in a bush; ≥ 3 m apart. Each candidate is "risky": inside some keeper's sight for 15–60 % of the stage cycle (tested). Eaten at centre distance ≤ 0.8 m; eaten snacks stay eaten after a catch; snacks of a left stage are lost.
- **Stages**: the panda's stage = the box containing its centre. Crossing a gate line moves the current stage on (stepping back moves it back); the gate's drop bar (0.2 m thick, on the line) falls only when the centre is 0.6 + 0.1 + 0.5 = **1.2 m** past the line, so it never overlaps the panda: then **no re-entry**. On the first entry only: bundles reset to 2, "Stage k" toast, `chime`. **Entry safety** (tested at every phase): the entry band (the gate's centre window × 1.5 m past the line) is outside every next-stage cone ∧ sight and ≥ 3.75 m (walking hearing + 0.75) from every next-stage keeper, so crossing never forces a catch. No pause, no teleport; the old stage's keepers keep walking but no longer detect.
- **Catch** (`CATCH`): −5 s on the clock, catches + 1. The 3rd catch ends the run (`lose`). Otherwise a 1.2 s opaque **caught card** (DOM `Hud`, input ignored, clock running): the panda is back at the current stage's start, every keeper of that stage drops its investigation and is put back at its **scheduled** route position (`undisturbed(τ)`: the patrol it would have walked without the panda), meters 0, bundles back to 2. Then 1.0 s **grace** (no meter fill, no bump, no hearing); the run's first second has the same grace. Respawn safety (tested at every phase): every stage start is outside every cone ∧ sight and ≥ 4 m from every keeper.
- **Clock** (`CLOCK`): 180 s in `rules.ts` (no `durationMs`, because catches change it); `timeLeft −= dt` each step, −5 per catch, shown as the HUD stat "Time".

## Scoring

`score = max(0, (won ? 1000 + 10 · floor(timeLeft) : 0) + 50 · snacks − 100 · catches)`. Live: `setScore(liveScore(run))` on **every** event (snack, catch, and right before `end("timeup")` / `end("lose")`), `liveScore = max(0, 50 · snacks − 100 · catches)`, so the chip never drifts from the formula (an `addScore(50)` after a floored 0 would) and never shows a negative; on the win `setScore(final)` and `end("win")` in the same callback. `finalScore` not overridden (duration = `elapsedMs`). Changed from spec ("−100 per catch (min 0)"): the floor applies to the whole sum, so a live chip never shows a negative.

### Server limits and why they hold

Proposed `meta.scoring` = `arcade-games.json` (Claude sets both in the go-live PR; `enabled: false` until then):

| | stub / provisional | proposed |
|---|---|---|
| `maxScore` / `max_score` | 4500 | **3290** |
| `minDurationMs` – `maxDurationMs` | 20000–200000 | **5000–182000** |
| `base` / `maxPointsPerSec` (`max_pps`) | 4500 / 4500 | **0 / 165** |

Proof (each rate cites a constant; tests through the real store):

1. **Speed**: no step moves the panda more than `3.2 · dt` (20,000 random steps, collisions, clamps, gates; respawns apart). A respawn can move it **forward by ≤ 1.5 m** (stages 2–3 start 1.5 m past G1 / G2 and the panda may be caught on the line) = 0.47 s of walking, and it costs ≥ 1.2 s of caught card, so a catch never makes a win earlier.
2. **Route floor** `MIN_ROUTE`: the win needs the centre to pass, in order, the windows G1 (x ∈ [3.5, 4.5] at z 0), G2 (x ∈ [−5.5, −4.5] at z −20), G3 (x ∈ [5.5, 7.5] at z −42) from the start (−4.5, 16.5). Each leg at least the straight line between its nearest window points (independent choices, so the sum is a floor): 18.34 + 21.54 + 24.17 = **64.04 m** (obstacles and the bridge only make it longer). Earliest win ≥ 64.04 / 3.2 = **20.01 s**; a respawn only adds distance.
3. **Bound** `B(t)`: a win at t scores ≤ 1000 + 50 · 12 + 10 · floor(180 − t) ≤ 1000 + 600 + 1590 = **3190** at 20.01 s and falls with t; a lose scores ≤ max(0, 600 − 300) = 300; a time-up ≤ 600 − 0 and only at elapsed ≥ 170 s (clock 180 − 5c, c ≤ 2). **Max points per second at every moment**: live score rises only by snacks, 50 each; centres ≥ 3 m apart minus two 0.8 m reaches = 1.4 m → ≥ 0.44 s → ≤ 114/s (harmless: the server checks only the final score, one event at the end).
4. **Line** `0 + 165 · t`: at 20.01 s it is 3302 ≥ 3190 (3.5 % margin) and grows while `B` falls; at the earliest lose (below) 891 ≥ 300. `maxScore` 3290 = 3190 + 3.1 %.
5. **Durations**: earliest end = a lose: every catch needs ≥ 1.0 s (run start or grace) of play since the last spawn, and two caught cards of 1.2 s lie between three catches: ≥ 3 · 1.0 + 2 · 1.2 = **5.4 s** → `minDurationMs` 5000. The clock reaches 0 by elapsed 180 s at the latest (it loses ≥ dt per frame, exactly `elapsedMs`) → `maxDurationMs` 182000.
6. **Bots** (200 seeds each, 30 / 60 / 144 Hz and random 4–50 ms frames, through `createArcadeStore` + `simulateRun`): every final passes `withinServerLimits`, `capScore` is a no-op, and the oracle reaches ≥ 90 % of `maxScore` (≥ 2961: a win by ≤ 43 s with all 12 snacks). These limits hold **only with** fix items 1, 5 and 6 (gates, live score, proof text) and the oracle ≥ 2961; otherwise the fallback `maxScore = max over k of [1000 + 50k + 10 · floor(180 − T_min(k))]` + 3 % (`T_min(k)` = the proven fastest win with k snacks: exact visibility-graph route round the inflated solids, the bridge and k snacks), never a change to the play.

## Run end

- `end("win")` on the step the panda's centre crosses G3 (z ≤ −42 inside the opening), with `setScore(final)` first, on the rules' own clock (the shell has no timer, so the last frame's points always count). `resultDelayMs: 1500`: gate fanfare, confetti, the panda's hop through the gate.
- `end("lose")` on the step of the 3rd catch (keeper "!", the panda squashes; `resultDelayMs` shows it).
- `end("timeup")` only when the rules' clock reaches 0 with the panda still inside: a real failure. Order in a step: clock first (a crossing on the frame the clock empties is a time-up); a catch whose −5 s empties the clock is a time-up unless it is the 3rd catch (`lose` wins).

## Scene and camera

- **Follow camera, 3/4 top-down, pitch 62° (1.082 rad), one yaw `[0]`**: the camera sits on +z of its aim and looks −z, so the escape always runs up the screen and `inputToWorld(…, view.yaw)` is fixed for the run. Why: the cones are fans on the floor and read best from high above, and one yaw keeps "up = north = towards the exit" on every screen.
- `camera.ts` `viewFor(width, height)` (pure, pinned in `camera.test.ts`): a **window** box `±hx × [0, 1.8] × ±hz` around the origin, fitted by `useFittedView({ area, pitch, yaws: [0], focus: [origin], margin: { top: 0.1, bottom: 0.06, left: 0.02, right: 0.02 }, padding: 8, shift: true, fov: 45 })`, which dollies to the closest distance that keeps the window clear of the HUD, the touch controls and the cookie banner. Starting windows: phone portrait (shorter side < 600) 8.5 × 12 m; phone landscape 16 × 7.5 m; larger screens 16 × 14 m (stage widths 14–20 m). Tuned by measurement, not prediction (dino-egg-rescue's predicted sizes were twice the measured ones).
- `<CameraRig follow={aim} followFraction={1} bounds={stageBounds} offset={view.offset} shift={view.shift} damping={5}>`: `aim` is a live point (panda x, 0, panda z − 1.5: 1.5 m look-ahead north), `stageBounds` = the current stage box shrunk by hz on the **south** and hx on the **sides** only (a line or point when the window is wider), with the north bound at gate z + hz − 3 (z grows south), so the window never shows past the stage's south or side edges and shows at most 3 m past the north gate. No `followFocus`: the window travels with the aim, so `focus` is the origin (treasure-island pattern). Changed from spec §5 ("fitted to the stage with `followFocus`"): a whole 14–20 × 18–22 m stage on a phone draws the panda and keepers far under 24 px; the window keeps them readable. CameraRig eases position and look-at by `1 − e^(−5 dt)`: a walking panda lags ≈ 3.2 / 5 = 0.64 m behind the aim, and a stage switch (bounds jump) glides in ≈ 0.6 s; `hz` keeps ≥ 2 m around the panda for both.
- **Readability (acceptance, measured in the build with `?perf=1` + a bot, banner open)**: the shorter side of the projected bounding box of the panda mesh, of every on-screen keeper's **0.9 m ground disc + meter ring** (the keeper's readable size: from 62° its own shorter side is only 0.35–0.55 m, 12–18 px landscape, 15–24 px portrait; scaling keepers up instead is Q6) and of every snack (its 0.9 m glow disc) ≥ 24 CSS px, min and median, at 390 × 844 and 844 × 390 (touch) and 1280 × 800; every keeper cone within 10 m of the panda is on screen or arrowed. Expected: portrait ≈ 44 px/m (panda 0.73 m wide ≈ 32 px), landscape ≈ 33 px/m on the ground (≈ 24 px): the landscape window shrinks first if a measurement fails. `<TargetMarkers>` arrows (fixed array of 5): the current exit gate (mint) and each current-stage keeper and the searchlight when off screen or under the HUD (amber; coral while its meter > 0).
- **Panda**: `<Model asset={ASSETS.panda}>` in a group at the rules' x/z and facing; `core/motion` `waddle(phase, 0.12, offset)` (phase += distance / 0.55 m · π) for roll / yaw / bob, sneak crouch `squashStretch(0.82, scale)` eased in 0.15 s, `hop(phase, 0.25, offset)` over 0.3 s (one `BodyOffset` and one scale scratch, reused) on a toss and through the final gate. In a bush its ears show over the leaves (panda 1.0 m tall, bush 0.9 m).
- **Keepers**: `<HumanoidModel asset={ASSETS.keeper} pose attach={{ handR: <Torch /> }}>` (applyLift default; the torch is one merged mesh with vertex colours, 1 draw call). `useHumanoidPose`: `walkPose(phase, amount)` with amount = min(1, speed / 3) and phase by `gaitPhaseStep(amount, ZOO_KEEPER_LANDMARKS, scale, speed, dt, 4)`; `idlePose` blended in when still; game-local `poses.ts` `torchPose`: the right arm aimed forward and slightly down with `aimArm(out, -1, …)` (blended through `carryPose(0)` so it never swings through the T); the look-around: the body faces `lookYaw` and `turnBone(out, BONE.chest, 0, 0.4 d, 0)` + `turnBone(out, BONE.head, 0, 0.6 d, 0)` with d = `agent.yaw − lookYaw`, so the head and torch follow the cone. "?" / "!" bubbles and the meter ring billboard above the head, every keeper's in shared instanced meshes (ring, fill, "?", "!": 4 draw calls in all), plus an instanced khaki ground disc (1 call).
- **Cones**: one `core/kit` `<Flashlight>` per keeper (`halfAngle` 30°, `range` 7 / 9, `height` 1.1, `color` `#fde68a`, `opacity` 0.25, `light={false}`), group placed on the feet with `rotation.y = agent.yaw` in a plain `useFrame` (after `useRunFrame`, this frame's state); colour `#f87171` while its meter > 0 (a React state set only when the calm / alert flag flips). Searchlight: `<Flashlight halfAngle={12°} range={14} height={4.5} light>` (the only SpotLight, mounted with the scene, skipped on "low" by the kit). Changed from spec ("cones as one batched mesh"): one Flashlight per mounted cone (2 draw calls each, ≤ 4 mounted = ≤ 8 calls) because the kit's fan is already tested equal to `inViewCone`. **It ignores blockers today**, so core addition C1 (occluded fan) is required for "cones match the logic exactly".
- **Mounted keepers**: the current stage's always; a next-stage keeper (with its cone) only while inside the camera window + 1 m (it cannot detect yet); the old stage's never (behind the south bound). At most one next-stage route enters that 4 m band past each gate, so **≤ 3 skinned keepers** at any time (spec kept; 2 + 1 at G2) and ≤ 4 Flashlights (stage 3: 3 + searchlight). The searchlight's Flashlight (its SpotLight) is mounted all run and never hidden, so the light count never changes.
- **Lighting**: `environment: { background: "#bae6fd", fog: ["#bae6fd", 30, 70], lighting: "day" }`; on entering stage 3 `useStageLighting("sunset", 1.5)` (core addition C2) fades to dusk, the lamps' emissive heads turn on. Without C2: stage 3 stays `day` with a dusk-tinted ground and sky band (Changed from spec, recorded then).
- Palette: grass `#4ade80`, paths `#e7e5e4`, asphalt `#475569`, cones `#fde68a` 25 %, alert `#f87171`, accent `#34d399`.

## Test plan

`rules.test.ts` ≤ ~600 lines, behaviour over branches; `fairness.test.ts` separate (heavier).

- **Pins**: every constant block and `C1..C3` cycles; `stepPatrol` routes return to their start after exactly `C` (1e−9).
- **Vision**: cone edge at exactly 30° and at range 7 / 9 visible, 30° + ε and range + ε not; walls, kiosks, cars, trunks and bushes block, benches / fences do not; a panda inside a bush is never seen from any of 2,000 keeper poses; property test, 20,000 random (keeper, panda) pairs: seen ⇒ `inViewCone ∧ hasLineOfSightXZ ∧ !hidden` and never through a blocker.
- **Hearing**: 3.0 m walking heard, 3.01 not; 1.2 / 1.21 sneaking; still panda never heard; rustle in a bush heard; 2 s cooldown.
- **Suspicion**: catch at exactly 1.2 s (1.5 coarse) of continuous sight; decay 0.5/s; brief glimpses add up only as the meter says; "?" → investigate last seen point, wait 2.5 s, return to route and resume the saved waypoint; targets clamped to the zone; no keeper ever inside a solid (20,000 steps with random investigations); bump and bush search catch at once.
- **Toss**: lands 4 m ahead, clamped short of a solid and of the stage edge; lands at 0.6 s; nearest keeper within 10 m (tie lower index) goes; a suspicious keeper ignores it; re-target; nobody in range; 2 per stage, reset on entry and on a catch; bamboo in the panda's bush finds it.
- **Searchlight**: sweep formula; sight rule; cars block; partial sight sends the nearest keeper within 12 m.
- **Stages and catches**: gate crossing switches stage, drop bar blocks re-entry, old keepers stop detecting; catch −5 s, card 1.2 s, respawn at the stage start, keepers back on schedule, meters 0, grace 1.0 s; 3rd catch `lose`; clock-emptying catch `timeup`; clock-first order on the last frame.
- **Ends and scoring**: `end("win")` on the crossing step with `setScore` first, never `"timeup"` for a win; score formula for every event; floor at 0; snack counts capped at 12; determinism (same seed and inputs → same run).
- **Fairness** (`fairness.test.ts`, C5): per stage, grid 0.4 m, step 0.25 s, sneak moves (4 neighbours = 1.6 m/s) and waits. Per keeper a **blocked mask** (cells × 108 / 96 / 72 phases) is precomputed once from its undisturbed patrol: a cell is blocked at step p when, at any of 8 sub-samples of [p, p + 1] (the look-around sweeps up to ≈ 1.3 rad/s and the yaw snaps at corners), the panda (r 0.5 + 0.75) is in the cone ∧ sight (angle + 3°), within 1.95 m (sneak hearing 1.2 + 0.75) or bumping; a move needs source and destination free over the step. A seeded offset k is a shift by 18 / 16 / 12 · k steps; a combination = OR of shifted masks (all 6 / 36 / 216 in seconds). **For every combination and phase index, every cell of the stage start and of the gate-entry band is winning** (safe route to the gate, no toss); respawn and entry safety at every phase; every snack candidate in sight 15–60 % of the cycle and on some winning route.
- **Degenerate bots must NOT score well** (200 seeds each): idle (0 points, `timeup` at 180 s, never caught); bush camper (walks to the safest bush and stays: never wins, ≤ its snacks · 50); sneak-forever solver (wins every seed with 0 catches, scores below the oracle); toss spammer (throws on every frame: 2 per stage, no score); catch farmer (walks into cones: score only falls, `lose` by 3); rusher (straight line, ignores keepers: bounded, **win rate ≤ 20 %**). **Novice proxy** (sneaks near keepers, waits in bushes, reacts 0.3 s late, heads greedily for the gate): **wins ≥ 50 % of seeds** (backs the DoD's novice playtest). **Solver bot** (C5 plan, through the real rules and store): wins every seed with 0 catches, proving the safe route in play, not only on the grid. **Oracle** (forward earliest-arrival search on the same masks with walk moves, 2-cell hops where walking hearing + 0.75 is clear, and the best of the 24 orders of each stage's 4 snacks): ≥ 90 % of `maxScore` (≥ 2961) on its best seed. Every run of every bot at 30 / 60 / 144 Hz and random frames passes `withinServerLimits`; `capScore` a no-op; pause and retry change nothing. If the file exceeds 60 s the oracle drops to 60 seeds (recorded here).
- `camera.test.ts`: `viewFor` windows, yaw 0 forward = −z, the aim never leaves the stage bounds; projected sizes at the three viewports as a pure check of the fit (the browser measurement is the acceptance). `poses.test.ts` / `assets.test.ts`: soles within 1 cm through walk and look-around, torch within 1 cm of the hand anchor, no arm through the T, fitted sizes. Browser: the common criteria (03), banner open and closed, Retry ×10 `geometries` flat.
