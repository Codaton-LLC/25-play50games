# Alien Farm

Owner: Codex (reassigned by the user, 2026-10-09). Slug: `alien-farm`. Adventure game 10, complexity 3. G0 design for P-14; spec: `03-game-specs-adventure.md` §10; assets: `05-hyper3d-catalog.md` §E.4. Units: metres, seconds, radians; x east, z south, y up. All gameplay constants belong in pure `rules.ts`; every departure is identified below.

## Concept

A mint alien tends nine moonlit plots, reads their rising pulse, plucks at peak glow and carries up to four crops to a moving saucer. Rhythm chooses when to harvest; the saucer route chooses when to leave. Maximise points over 100 played seconds; complete the shipment goal for a win. Soil `#3b0764`, mint `#4ade80`, cyan `#22d3ee`, purple rocks `#7c3aed`, sky `#020617`; accent `#4ade80`.

## Controls

`meta.ts` exact `controls`: `scheme: "joystick"`, `keyboard: "WASD / arrows to move; press E, Enter or Space to harvest at peak glow; enter the beam to deliver"`, `touch: "Joystick to move; tap Harvest at peak glow; enter the beam to deliver"`. `index.tsx`: `touchControls: ["joystick", "action"]`, `touchLabels: { action: "Harvest" }`.

- `moveX/moveY` → `inputToWorld(moveX, moveY, view.yaw, out)`, magnitude clamped to 1. Harvest reads `actionPressed || jumpPressed` once at the beginning of the played frame (E/Enter/Harvest or Space); simultaneous keys are one attempt. Held `action/jump` never repeat; no canvas tap, pointer aim, jump or drag action. Release before another attempt. Esc/P and hidden-tab pause are shell-owned.
- Changed from spec: arrows/Enter are documented because core already maps them; the touch label says Harvest rather than Action so instructions match the button. Coarse-pointer mode is sampled once at run creation, disclosed in help and fixed through that run.

## Rules

Named groups: `FARM`, `FARMER`, `PLOTS`, `CYCLE`, `HARVEST`, `SHIP`, `SPORES`, `GOAL`, `SCORE`. No Rapier, wall-clock timers or mesh-derived gameplay collision.

- **Farm:** visual crater 26×20; walkable rectangle x±10.5/z±8.5, farmer centre inset by radius 0.4 to x±10.1/z±8.1. Start (0,6), velocity 0, facing north (yaw pi). Plot ids are row-major, x = −5,0,5 and z = −4,0,4. Each raised bed is 1.6×1.6, top y0.08; beds/channels are walkable, not obstacles. Six rocks at (±12,−7/0/7), size 1.2; three 0.8 crates at (−11.8,8.8),(−10.8,9.2),(−11.8,7.8), all outside walkable bounds, decoration only. No layout rejection or secret obstacles.
- **Movement:** radius 0.4, acceleration 24, braking 30, facing `turnTowards` at 14 rad/s. Carry n=0..4 gives top speeds `4.5*(1−0.08*n)` = 4.5/4.14/3.78/3.42/3.06. A spore zone multiplies these by 0.6; overlaps never multiply again. Cap velocity immediately when a load/zone lowers the limit; clamp centre, then cap final displacement to the active speed×dt. Harvest plants feet for 0.30 s with velocity 0. No pushing, knockback or teleporting.
- **Activation/types:** ids0,2,4,6,8 activate at a=0; ids1,7 at a=20; ids3,5 at a=40. First seven are glowPods (C=6); the last two are gourds (C=8). Inactive beds show a locked seed icon/countdown, have no crop, attempts or spores. Changed from spec: define the unspecified progression explicitly; gourds appear on newly activated beds so an existing crop never changes rhythm mid-cycle.
- **Seed:** one `createRng(seed)` draws nine offsets δ=2*rng(), then route direction (rng()<0.5 ? +1 : −1) and start distance s0=64*rng(). Draw in that order, including offsets for inactive plots. Cycle k starts at `b=a+δ+k*C` (k≥0); before the first b show a waiting sprout, with no attempt effect. Cycle interval is [b,b+C), peak p=b+C/2. Cycles use absolute played time; picking, missing and spores do not shorten/rephase them. Retry creates fresh state with a new Scene seed, never carries inventories/events over; explicit same-seed tests reproduce the schedule.
- **Growth:** normal half-window w is 0.90 if peak p<40, 0.75 if 40≤p<70, 0.60 thereafter, chosen for the entire cycle from p. Sprout [b,b+0.2C), growing [b+0.2C,p−w), ripe [p−w,p+w] inclusive, overripe (p+w,b+0.85C), burst at b+0.85C, spent until b+C. Successful harvest replaces the remaining cycle with harvested/spent and prevents its burst. A burst happens once even across a long frame. Perfect means abs(attemptTime−p)≤0.25, or ≤0.35 in coarse mode; every perfect is inside ripe. An invalid early attempt locks harvesting for that cycle but growth and the eventual burst still happen.
- **Attempt:** each harvest press first samples whether cooldown was expired, then refreshes cooldown to attemptTime+0.60, including presses during channel/cooldown or with no target. A blocked press is discarded, never queued; tapping faster than0.60 s keeps the tool cooling. For an unblocked press, choose nearest active, unharvested crop centre within inclusive0.90 m (ties lower id); with this grid only one can be in range. Capacity must be<4 and channel idle. No target, waiting/spent bed or full hands: no crop mutation or score. Outside ripe, mark that crop cycle missed: no retry until its next fixed cycle. Otherwise reserve that crop/slot, snapshot normal/perfect at the press and face its centre, then automatically finish the planted0.30 s channel; no hold required. Reservation suppresses burst/reset until completion; the shortest ripe tail still leaves>0.30 s before burst. Award/append only at completion; deadline cancels an unfinished channel without points.
- Changed from spec: outside-window attempts consume that cycle's opportunity; successful harvests plant feet for0.30 s; EVERY press refreshes the0.60 s cooldown. This makes mistiming visible and defeats holding/rapid retry without penalising a normal route (minimum travel+pick gap already exceeds0.60). Help states “Wait for the ring; an early pick misses this crop. Repeated taps keep the tool cooling.” A small cooldown arc is always visible after a press.
- **Inventory:** fixed four slots store plot id, cycle id and perfect flag; the crop's quality never changes in transit. No drops, selling, spoilage or discard button; full hands cannot harm a waiting crop. A visual basket holds four miniature crops; carry speed uses occupied slots, not delivered total. Perfect streak is feedback only: increment on perfect harvest, reset on normal/failed eligible attempt; delivery leaves it unchanged. No streak points.
- **Saucer:** closed nonsmooth `createPath` polyline through (−9,−7),(9,−7),(9,7),(−9,7), y0 for rules, exact length64. Body hovers at y3.2; beam centre is path XZ, radius2 inclusive for the farmer CENTRE (do not add farmer radius). `advance` by signed distance: 2 m/s before40, 2.5 at40..70, 3 thereafter; split at speed boundaries, no reset/jump. Seeded s0/direction are the only route variation. Corners intentionally have continuous position and instantaneous tangent change; body has no collision.
- **Load:** after movement/harvest completion, if n>0 and centre distance≤2, atomically unload ALL n, add delivery bonus, update delivered/perfect-delivered counters and source-plot bitmask, then clear slots. No dwell/button/entry latch: entering with new cargo while already in beam is legal. Empty checks pay zero; each cargo id can unload once. Moving farmer and beam are checked at each ≤1/120 s substep; no swept bonus or retroactive boundary sample. Minimum plot-to-route distance is3: 3−0.90=2.10>beam radius2, so a stationary harvester cannot also load. Ring/arrow show current beam position and direction, not a false fixed depot.
- **Spores:** every missed/unharvested burst creates a radius2 XZ zone centred on that plot for [burstTime,burstTime+5), independent of the next crop. One slot per plot suffices because C≥6>5. Farmer centre inside inclusive radius2 slows; zones never block harvest/beam or spread. Existing zones remain across cycle reset. Cloud is cosmetic and translucent above the bed; the ground hazard disc persists with reduced motion.
- **Clock/order:** omit `durationMs`; rules own the 100 s deadline, HUD Time `ceil(max(0,100−t))`. One `useRunFrame` consumes played dt through core `substep`≤1/120, splitting at deadline, activation, cycle/window/burst/expiry, cooldown/channel and ship-speed boundaries; keep all remainder before deadline. At an equal timestamp: deadline first; cycle/activation/expiry transitions; channel completion; input attempt (once/frame); movement; loading; event flush. Ripe endpoints belong to ripe; apply overripe only strictly after its endpoint. Score events flush before `end`; no rules mutate during pause/over/result delay. Store time may exceed rules'100 by <50 ms on the final frame.
- **Goal:** at 100 s, win requires ≥16 delivered crops, ≥6 distinct DELIVERED source plots and ≥4 delivered perfects. Reaching the goal early does not end the score run. Carried crops earn harvest points but do not satisfy delivery goals; no automatic final unload. No health/lives/early loss. Changed from spec: the pure time-up score attack gains a shipment completion goal and explicit timed win/lose; rules own the clock so the last played fraction/points survive and a successful run gets the win cue.
- **Human feasibility estimate, not measurement:** four full loads with 4/16 perfects score2600. Budget about100 m travel at effective2.5 m/s (allowing cargo/spore slowdown)=40 s, sixteen planted picks4.8 s, rhythmic waiting20 s, route/beam alignment12 s and sixteen0.5 s reaction allowances8 s: about85 s, leaving15 s for detours/misses. Six-plot coverage requires two rows; no optional mechanic is needed. Fine/coarse perfect windows span0.50/0.70 s; normal windows remain1.2–1.8 s. Build acceptance requires delayed-input keyboard/touch witnesses reaching the goal by85 s and actual human wins, not just an instantaneous oracle.

## Scoring

`runScore = 50*harvested + 50*perfectHarvested + 25*sum(loadSize²)`. Load size is1..4; perfect doubles only the crop's50, never its delivery bonus. Live `addScore(50|100)` at channel completion, `addScore(25*n*n)` per atomic unload; final `setScore(runScore)` reconciles, then `end`. No terminal/time/streak/activation/burst points, deductions or `finalScore` override. Pending cargo keeps its harvest points on loss. HUD: Time, Delivered n/16, Sources n/6, Perfect delivered n/4, Carry n/4; values display actual totals beyond goals.

### Server limits and why they hold (the proof)

Exact proposed `meta.scoring = { maxScore: 24800, minDurationMs: 99000, maxDurationMs: 102000, base: 200, maxPointsPerSec: 250 }`. Claude pairs these same five values in `arcade-games.json` before go-live; status dev/enabled false until accepted. This is a conservative legal ceiling, not an achievable oracle score.

1. Each harvested id is one plot-cycle, paid once. Earliest normal completion is3−0.90+0.30=2.40 s (initial glowPod δ=0); no crop can pay sooner. Perfects, later activations and gourds only delay it.
2. Consecutive harvests at different plots require ≥4−2*0.90=2.20 m centre travel, at maximum real speed4.5, PLUS the next planted0.30 s: gap g≥2.20/4.5+0.30=71/90 s. Cargo/spore speeds, acceleration, cooldown and beam detours only increase it; no movement occurs during the channel. Same-plot consecutive cycles have attempt separation≥6−2*0.90=4.20 s, also >g. Transfers to another plot cannot change that inequality.
3. For H≥1 completions, t≥2.40+(H−1)*71/90. H=124 needs≥99.433333 s; H=125 needs≥100.222222>deadline. Thus H≤124, for every seed/control/frame schedule. Independent cycle ceiling: five initial glowPods×17, two later×13, two gourds×8 =127, looser than travel/service. Prove both to catch activation/cycle mutations.
4. Every prefix has harvest points≤100H. For disjoint delivered loads n≤4, n²≤4n and delivered≤H, so delivery points≤100H. Therefore ALL live prefixes/terminal scores≤200H≤24800, including harvest and load occurring in one substep. End adds zero; carried, failed and unharvested items add no hidden bonus.
5. `200H ≤ 200+250*(2.40+(H−1)*71/90)` for integers1..124; smallest margin258.333 points at124. H=0 is trivial. Store played time≥simulation time; nearest-ms submission rounding costs≤0.125 points, inside that margin. This proves the rate envelope at every prefix, not just terminal100 s. Both terminal reasons occur at store100000..100050 ms (exclusive upper edge), inside99000..102000; countdown/pause/result delay are excluded.
6. Tests pin literals, enumerate H1..125 and cycle ceilings, assert prefix max/rate plus `capScore` unchanged, and use `withinServerLimits` on every terminal result (its duration minimum intentionally does not apply to live prefixes). Legal oracle uses real movement/carry/spores/channel/beam and full loads, never teleports, resets phases, freezes ship or invents input. Record extrema, deliveries/perfects and goal time; no executed bot results claimed at G0.

## Run end

- At rules t=100: first retain all events strictly before the deadline, reconcile score, then `end("win")` iff all three goal conditions hold, otherwise `end("lose")`. Deadline wins ties with harvest/loading; no `"timeup"` path or shell timer. A completed goal at99.99 remains a win at100 even if carrying extra crops.
- `resultDelayMs: 1600`; win freezes simulation, turns the farmer to camera with `cheerPose` and confetti; loss has a gentle arms-down shrug. Shell owns end stings, results, submission, Retry/Exit; never call API/localStorage. Animation during result delay cannot score.
